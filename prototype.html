Two ideas for the quizzes

Both of these are playable. Nothing here is in the app yet — try them and say which is worth building.

Idea one

The round draws a constellation

Every question is a star. Answering lights it and draws the line to the last one, so by the end of the round you have made a shape. Get one wrong and the star still lights, just dimmer — nothing breaks, and nothing is taken away.

Idea two

The topic map

A topic is seven lessons and a review — a finish line you can actually see. Each stop fills in as you meet its questions. Nothing is locked: you can open any lesson whenever you like, the map just shows where you have been.

For Chris — what these deliberately are not
  • Nothing is currency. No points to spend, no shop, nothing unlockable. The crest records that a topic happened; it cannot be bought or traded.
  • Nothing breaks. A missed question still lights its star, and a half-finished topic just sits there. There is no streak to lose and no disappointed face.
  • Neither rewards time spent, which would quietly reward dawdling. Both reward finishing something and coming back.
  • Both are derived from records the app already writes — question coverage and round results. No new counters, nothing extra to sync.
