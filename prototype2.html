For Chris — what this does and does not change
  • Her constellation roster survives intact. The Otter, The Paper Boat, The Compass, The Lantern, The Dolphin's Leap — half of them are river creatures already. The Sky Map becomes a river she has charted, with the same names and the same lore. No rewriting.
  • Blue, not teal. Ad Astra owns teal-black; these two must never read as the same app. The accent stays Blossom pink because she picked it, and pink on deep water is the point rather than a compromise.
  • No mechanic changes. Stars still light for showing up, misses still light dimmer, nothing is currency and nothing is gated. This is paint and layout.
  • The tool row is the real usability win — four stacked full-width buttons under every question is roughly 250px of furniture between her and the next question.
