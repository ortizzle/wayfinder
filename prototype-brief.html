A note from home

Big Friday coming — but you've got all week. One thing a night. We're proud of you. 💛

Two decisions this prototype is making, both worth arguing with.
  • The week card gave up its action rows. It used to carry up to three doors ("12 review questions due in Accelerated Math ›"). The brief now says the same thing with more context, so the card above is reduced to the five-day strip and its summary line. Leaving both would print the same three counts twice, one screen apart — the exact redundancy the runway and the Growth tile already yield to each other over.
  • The brief sits above Coming up, not below. It's the thing that asks something of her; Coming up is reference. And the post-it stays at the very bottom, so the scroll passes everything that matters on the way to it.
Where every number comes from — nothing here is invented.
  • Tests and days: weekAhead(date), already shipped.
  • Questions and cards: open units per subject — units(cid) filtered by unitDone(), the same coverage rule the shelf uses.
  • Review due: dueMisses() filtered by subject.
  • Minutes: quizLimit() — her real measured seconds-per-question (clamped 8–40s, 20s until she's answered enough to measure). Cards derived the same way from flashcard sessions.
  • Which subject lands on which day: heaviest and neediest first, using the same KIND_WEIGHT + accuracy tie-break weekAhead already uses to pick the week's focus subject.
  • CJ / Schoology / notes: fixed copy. The app doesn't know whether her CJ is up to date and must never pretend to — it's a reminder, not a checkbox that tracks her.
