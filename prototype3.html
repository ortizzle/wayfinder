Surfaces, not outlines

The same screen twice. On the left, today's style: a 1px border on every box. On the right, the proposal: borders only on things you can press — everything else separates by tone and room.

Today — bordered
Proposed — tonal
What the rule would be
  • A border means "you can press this". Answer options, tools, and chips keep theirs — it is the affordance. Static cards lose theirs and sit one tone above the page instead.
  • Nothing gets lighter to read. Text tokens and every measured contrast ratio are untouched; only box outlines change.
  • Subject colour still anchors. The left-edge subject bars, the painted tiles, and the accent rules all stay exactly as they are.
  • If it ships, it ships measured — the usual sweep across accent × sky × theme, plus screenshots of every screen in both apps.
